# Budda's photo sources

Retrieved 2026-10-12 for the reference redesign. These files are public photographs published on Budda's previous official website, [buddasbakery.store](https://buddasbakery.store/), which identifies the Pleasant Grove business at 205 E 700 S and (801) 701-0617. They are not generated images or stock substitutes. Published media metadata is available at `https://buddasbakery.store/wp-json/wp/v2/media?per_page=100`.

All three local files were visually inspected after download. The photographed rolls are sweet bakery rolls, unlike the sushi-like food in the supplied design reference.

No verified storefront photo was found for either Utah branch during the bounded source check; no building image was fabricated. The present official site's public food image URLs returned a Cloudflare challenge to shell downloads, so the accessible previous official site's photos were used.

`pleasant-grove-reference.png` and `salt-lake-city-reference.png` (each 116 × 135 pixels) reuse the storefront thumbnails from the user's attached `codex-clipboard-b7e07929-0084-4212-b5ed-e190b1e0d7f9.png`. They were captured without synthesizing or changing the building/signage, using the browser image viewer. They are low-resolution user-provided reference material, not independently verified current venue photographs. The full reference was served only temporarily for these captures and is removed from the public assets.

The previous official site's home HTML and [gallery footer](https://buddasbakery.store/gallery/) publish https://www.facebook.com/BuddasBakery and https://www.instagram.com/buddas_bakery/. Those destinations were identified from the restaurant's published links; the social account content was not inspected.
