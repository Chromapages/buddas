# Reference-inspired hero artwork

`hero-reference.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the user-selected mockup on 2026-10-12. It is promotional illustration, including the mock's handwritten copy and roll photography, rather than a photograph documenting the restaurant's actual menu or premises.

The generator was asked for only the hero asset, excluding navigation, buttons, other sections and device chrome. Output is 2022 × 778 pixels. Original logos and verified business contact data were not overwritten. Other sourced asset provenance is recorded separately in `asset-sources.md`.

`bento-reference.png` (1942 × 809) reproduces the supplied $9 Bento poster as promotional artwork. It may only replace artwork for an existing CMS offer whose title explicitly contains `$9 Bento`; it must not introduce a price or offer when the CMS does not provide one. `botanical-reference.png` (1254 × 1254) is transparent, low-contrast decorative leaf/hibiscus artwork from the same reference.

Generative attempts to reconstruct storefront thumbnails were rejected because they synthesized facade/signage details. The final storefront assets are unchanged browser captures of the photographs already visible in the user-supplied reference, not those generated outputs.
